BLOCKER [IoUringTransportLayer.java](/home/florent/kafka-current-london-2026/.claude/worktrees/io-uring/server/src/main/java/org/apache/kafka/network/iouring/IoUringTransportLayer.java:443): async write completion does not wake the selector.

Exact problem: the Netty `writeAndFlush` listener only decrements `pendingWriteBytes` and records `asyncWriteFailure`. It never calls back into `IoUringSelector.wakeup()`. `IoUringSelector.poll()` can then sleep at [IoUringSelector.java:545](/home/florent/kafka-current-london-2026/.claude/worktrees/io-uring/server/src/main/java/org/apache/kafka/network/iouring/IoUringSelector.java:545) even after the event loop has completed the write.

Failure scenario: a normal small response is queued from the Processor thread. In real Netty, off-event-loop `writeAndFlush` is scheduled onto the channel event loop, so the future may complete just after the next `poll()` observes `hasPendingWrites() == true` and goes to sleep. Because small writes do not necessarily trigger `channelWritabilityChanged`, nothing wakes the Processor. The channel stays muted until the 300 ms poll timeout, delaying `completedSends()`, `RESPONSE_SENT`, and unmute. This can impose 300 ms stalls on ordinary request/response flow.

Suggested fix: give `IoUringTransportLayer` a write-completion callback, passed from `IoUringSelector` as `wakeup::release`, and invoke it from the `ChannelFuture` listener after decrementing `pendingWriteBytes` and after recording async failure. Add a test with an asynchronously completed write future; `EmbeddedChannel`’s synchronous completion masks this.
